# Privacy Policy

**Oura Research Adherence Monitor**

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Oura Research Adherence Monitor is a software prototype under development to evaluate the feasibility of monitoring wearable data availability and adherence through the Oura API.

The application is currently intended exclusively for personal testing by its developer. It is not authorized or deployed for research participant monitoring.

## 2. Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access Oura data necessary to evaluate data availability and device synchronization, including:

- Daily sleep records and associated metrics
- Dates of available sleep records
- Information necessary to determine whether expected records are missing

The application will request only the permissions necessary for its intended functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Oura API will be used solely to develop, test, and evaluate the functionality of the adherence-monitoring prototype.

Data will not be sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third parties.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

During initial development, testing will be conducted in a local development environment.

Authentication credentials and access tokens will not be published in the public GitHub repository.

Any locally stored Oura data will be restricted to the development environment and protected using appropriate access controls.

The public repository will contain documentation, software code, and synthetic demonstration data rather than actual Oura account data.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data obtained during personal testing will be retained only as necessary for development and evaluation.

The account holder may discontinue testing, revoke application access, and request deletion of locally retained data.

## 6. Research Participant Data

The application is not currently approved for research participant use.

Any future use involving research participants will require appropriate institutional review and authorization, applicable research ethics approval, and updated privacy and data management documentation.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application develops. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this application or its privacy practices may be directed to the developer through the contact email provided in the Oura developer application registration.
